# Today (home): surface brief

Scope: the home route becomes "Today", the daily ritual screen; its world becomes the site-wide shell (nav, tokens, type) for later surfaces. Mode: experience.
Audience/job: the one student, every day: solve today's problem, run a focus session, see the streak and the countdown, resume where they left off. Constraints: ADHD (one focal thing at a time), colorful but not dark, no template feel, only public-domain/CC0 art ships.
Memorable moment: today's MAIN card dealt face-up on top of a fanned hand, and solving it right there.
Open: exact exam date (editable, stored); video source for the focus card (user-pasted YouTube or the default stream).

## Direction contract

THESIS: Each study day is one riso-printed zine page with the day dealt as collectible cards; it refuses the stat-tile dashboard and the row of equal cards.

OWN-WORLD: Cornflower paper stock (#79a6e0) with printer's grain; warm plum-black ink (#2b1a24) for every outline; cream card stock (#fdf3dc); deep cherry (#9e1f24) and tangerine (#f3a35a) as the two spot colors; hand-lettered chunky display (Gluten), rounded hand caps for labels (Patrick Hand SC), Atkinson Hyperlegible Next for reading; thick ink borders, offset ink shadows, pill buttons, tilted cards; public-domain engravings printed into card windows.

STORY: The student sees the date and how many days remain, sees their streak stamped on the month, and knows exactly one thing to do next: solve today's MAIN card, then focus.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left third: huge hand-lettered date with a cherry misprint shadow, cream countdown tag, stamped month grid. Right two-thirds: fanned hand (BONUS streak behind left, SIDE focus behind right, MAIN problem on top, largest, tilted -2°), primary "Solve it" cherry pill under the MAIN card.

FORM: grounded "Riso Card Zine", user-steered combo of round-one assigned direction and pick, cherry-on-cornflower stock; seed: de9b8004411279f3

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
